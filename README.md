# Crimes Against Women in India (2024)

## Overview

This project is a statistical study analyzing crimes against women across Indian states using official crime statistics. The objective is to understand state-wise variation in crime incidence and examine socio-economic factors influencing crime patterns.

The study focuses on the relationship between crime rates, poverty, and female education using statistical methods.

---

## Objectives

* Analyze state-wise variation in crimes against women
* Identify high-incidence states for different crime categories
* Study socio-economic determinants of crime
* Examine relationships between crime, poverty, and education
* Provide data-driven policy insights

---

## Data

The study uses official crime statistics from Indian states, including:

* Category-wise crime incidence against women
* Poverty indicators
* Female education levels
* State-level socio-economic variables

---

## Methodology

### Statistical Analysis

* Descriptive analysis of crime distribution across states
* Identification of high-incidence regions

### Correlation Analysis

* Partial correlation between crime, poverty, and education
* Control for socio-economic confounding variables

### Relationship Analysis

* Bidirectional relationship exploration between:

  * Crime and poverty
  * Crime and education
  * Poverty and education

---

## Key Contributions

* Performed state-wise comparative crime analysis
* Identified socio-economic patterns influencing crime incidence
* Applied partial correlation to control confounding effects
* Analyzed multi-dimensional relationships between key variables
* Derived policy-relevant insights for crime reduction strategies
